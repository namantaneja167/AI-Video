# Pip Explains production instructions

Before creating or revising an episode, scene, animation, cover, storyboard or video-production instruction, read [the brand standards](docs/studio/pip-brand-standards.md). They are the authority for the approved visual structure, identity and review reference. Follow the labelled stage-strip and full-page takeaway sections when planning and reviewing an episode, and **Presentation flexibility — approved 24 October 2026**: the header and strip appear where useful, close-ups and demonstrations may use more of the frame, and Pip may move, take part or leave a shot; existing episodes keep their presentation defaults.

For scripts, hooks, content strategy or profile copy, also read [.agents/product-marketing.md](.agents/product-marketing.md), which is shared by the installed marketing skills.

Build new topic-specific animations and components as needed within the brand. Existing scene types are starting points, not a limit on explanatory quality. Preserve approved Pip artwork/voice and frozen benchmarks.

When adopting an isolated review render, distinguish visual approval from Studio artifact approval. Record changed render sources through the supported workflow and render/review the resulting artifact; documentation changes alone do not integrate a scene or publish a post.

For new episodes or motion revisions, apply **Discovery workflow and richer motion — approved 22 October 2026** in the authoritative Studio standard: demonstrate the surprise immediately, plan richer continuous motion, and complete the hook animatic and takeaway checkpoint before full narration. This section refines spoken opening order while preserving series identity and author review.
