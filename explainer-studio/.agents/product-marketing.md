## Product overview
Pip Explains is a free educational Instagram channel, @pip.theengineer, making polished animated explanations of tech concepts and verified tech news. Pip is the original ivory-and-orange capsule mascot. Goal: build an understandable, recognisable, trusted educational brand and grow its audience. There is no paid offer or advertising budget currently.
